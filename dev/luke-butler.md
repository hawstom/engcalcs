The one place to look him up. Research about him lives where it was written; this file points at
it and logs contact with him, newest first.

## Who he is

- Author of the **epanet-js toolkit** (MIT, © 2019 Luke Butler): the browser build of the EPANET
  engine that `js/vendor/epanet-js.js` vendors. Credited by name on not-epanet.org and in
  librewaternet.org's `CLAIMS.md` row 4.8.
- Co-founder, with Sam Payá, of **Iterating Inc.** (Toronto, 2025), which makes the epanet-js web
  application (FSL-1.1-MIT, a different thing from the toolkit) and Mastering Water Models.
  The split is in `dev/reaction-rate-upstream.md`.
- Background, from secondary sources: a graduate at an Australian water utility, then a consultant
  automating modelling workflows in code.
- Disposition: Tom MacWright's account (macwright.com/2025/07/03/epanet-placemark) of Luke and Sam
  sending fixes back to Placemark voluntarily. That is the best evidence we have of good faith.
