# Outreach venues: advisors and proteges

Supporting research for ROADMAP Task 218. The commitment, the nagging instruction and the action log
live in the task; this file holds the venue list so the task stays short.

## The distinction that governs every action

**Advisors and proteges are two different lists, and no venue serves both.** Tom said plainly on
2026-08-05 that he did not understand this roadmap item, and conflating the two is probably why.

- An **advisor** here means someone with numerical/hydraulic depth who will answer a hard question
  six months from now. That is a peer relationship, earned by asking good technical questions in a
  developer community.
- A **protege** means someone learning to build tools like these, who needs a person willing to
  teach. That only ever comes from a room with students in it.

**Never plan one action hoping it produces both.** Every concrete next action must say which of the
two it is aimed at.

## Set expectations honestly, or the item keeps disappointing

A good OWA post yields two or three technically serious people who now know the project exists. It
does not yield a mentor, and it will not yield a protege at all. That is still worth having — but
"I posted and nothing happened" is the predictable failure mode if the expected return was never
stated.

**The pattern that works:** go where people are already doing the specific work these calculators
serve, not where the profession socializes.

Discussions**, `https://github.com/orgs/OpenWaterAnalytics/discussions`, category **Q&A**. Earlier
versions of Task 218 said "forum", then "repo Discussions"; both were wrong.

total. The codebase is active and the maintainers are real, but the discussion surface is not busy: a
reply may take weeks or never come, and **that is the expected case rather than a failure**. The
activity is in Issues, where this question does not belong. Worth one evening, not a substitute for
the other venues, and it **must not block Task 213** — the EPANET source is public and readable if no
answer arrives.
