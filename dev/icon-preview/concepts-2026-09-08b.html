Water-tower candidates, round 2 (2026-09-08b)

Round 1 was rejected whole. These are Tom's own towers, measured out of tgh-icon-concepts.png by a pixel scan rather than eyeballed, and mapped once into the suite's 24-unit frame. Every candidate is a single currentColor stroke, so a winner is a copy-paste into lib/Icons.lib.php. Each row is 16 / 24 / 32 / 64 px on light and on dark, then a nearest-neighbor blow-up of the 16 px raster, which is the favicon question answered by pixels.

wt-wideWT-WIDE, as drawn
FAVICON His flat-crowned dome, his bar catwalk overhanging both walls, his shallow bowl, his thick riser, and the wall that runs on as the leg. No ellipse, no joint, no splay. Reads at 16. Riser edges 10.4 and 13.6 against leg inner edges 4.1 and 19.9: 6.3 units of daylight, well over the 2.5-unit floor.
wt-wide-heavy-walkWT-WIDE, catwalk at 2.6
FAVICON He drew the catwalk at twice the outline weight, 10 px against 5. Two units is the set weight; 2.6 is as far as that emphasis carries before the bar eats the tank. Reads at 16, and the bar is the first thing the eye finds. It costs 0.3 unit of the bowl cavity at each end, which is still open.
wt-wide-long-walkWT-WIDE, catwalk out to the frame
FAVICON Overhang is the whole point of a catwalk (Tom, 2026-09-05). 1.4 to 22.6 instead of 2.2 to 21.8: 0.8 more each side, spent on the one feature that says the tank is serviced. Reads at 16, and the round cap ends land 0.4 unit inside the frame, so nothing clips. The bar now dominates the mark.
wt-wide-short-legsWT-WIDE, legs stopping short
FAVICON Round 1 measured that legs reaching the same ground line as the riser turn the tower into a table. Legs to 19.2, riser to 22.4, so only the pipe touches the ground. Reads at 16 and keeps the round-1 lesson. At 64 the tower looks slightly suspended.
wt-wide-thin-riserWT-WIDE, riser at 2.6
FAVICON His riser is 6x the outline weight, which maps to 3.7 units. 3.2 is the set precedent; 2.6 asks whether the thinner pipe still reads as a pipe. Reads at 16, but 0.6 unit of difference from a leg is not enough at that size and the pipe starts to read as a third leg, which is the shape a riser exists to avoid.
wt-wide-LWT-WIDE with an L
MENU ONLY L for Libre in the tank crown, the widest clear field the drawing has. Culled as a favicon. At 16 the L foot lands on the catwalk and the stem on the crown, so the tank fills with ink and reads as a scribble. It holds from 32 up.
wt-tall-3WT-TALL-3, as drawn
RECORD ONLY His aspect kept exactly: 85 px wide against 165 tall. Gable, plumb walls that run on as legs, box catwalk flattened to a bar, hemispherical bottom, riser at leg weight. Culled as a favicon. At his aspect the mark is 9.8 units wide, so at 16 px it holds 6.5 pixel columns and the catwalk overhang, the bowl and the riser all fall inside three of them. Riser to leg is 1.95 units, under the floor.
wt-tall-3-thick-riserWT-TALL-3, riser at 3.2
RECORD ONLY The one place his TALL-3 disagrees with his own WT-WIDE: there the riser is 6x a leg, here it is 1x. A riser at leg weight is a third leg. Culled as a favicon. The heavier pipe is the right call, but at his width it leaves 1.45 units to each leg and the three verticals fuse into one block at 16.
wt-tall-3-fitWT-TALL-3, fitted to the frame
FAVICON The x axis stretched 1.83x so the catwalk spans 2.8 to 21.2. Same vertical layout, same features, 1.9x the drawn area at any pixel size. Reads at 16 and is the best of the tall family: 4.8 units of riser-to-leg daylight, an open bowl, and a catwalk that clears both walls.
wt-tall-3-fit-short-legsWT-TALL-3 fitted, legs stopping short
FAVICON The round-1 truncation lesson applied to the fitted tower. Reads at 16. The truncation buys less here than on WT-WIDE, because the hemispherical bowl already separates the legs from the pipe.
wt-tall-3-fit-LWT-TALL-3 fitted, with an L
MENU ONLY L on the cylinder, which is a taller field than WT-WIDE offers. Culled as a favicon for the same reason as wt-wide-L, though the taller field lets the letter survive 24 px rather than needing 32.
wt-tall-1WT-TALL-1, as drawn, for comparison
RECORD ONLY What TALL-3 simplified away: a narrower tank, a shallow bowl instead of a hemisphere, and legs that leave the wall line and splay. Culled as a favicon. Narrower than TALL-3 and carrying splayed legs as well, so it loses on both counts. Kept to show what the simplification bought.
