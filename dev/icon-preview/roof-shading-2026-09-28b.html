Roof shading, round 2 (2026-09-28)

Tom on round 1: “The roof is not shaded conically. This is apparent at all sizes. The largest size is most obvious” (with a red arrow at the 512’s apex); “The top of the cylinder needs a seam, I believe. Check references”; and “The underside of the catwalk should be in shadow. Maybe move the catwalk down a little bit to meet the belly.”

Conical shading: round 1 pasted the wall’s horizontal gradient onto the roof, which gives vertical iso-brightness lines — correct for a cylinder, wrong for a cone. Round 2 replaces it with a fan of wedges from the apex to the base rim, each one flat colour sampled from the wall’s own ramp at its azimuth, so the shading converges at the apex like a real lit cone. Seam: a rim line (dark line, thin highlight above it) now runs at the wall/roof junction on every build, matching the top-angle/knuckle joint real welded tanks use there (references in the generator script’s header comment). Catwalk shadow: the deck moved down toward the bowl’s spring line, and both the underside band and the cast shadow on the bowl top are darker.
