The larger water tower: four ways to draw it

Tasks 679, 648 and 645, one piece of work. The favicon is not touched and is not on this page; every drawing here uses its coordinates. Each option is shown at the size it would really be used. Pick one per size, or one for all.

OptionWhat changes
1Nothing. What ships today.
2Only the outline, narrowed to suit the size: 0.6 of a unit at About (the weight of your own sketch), 0.3 at 192, 0.18 at 512, against the favicon’s 2. Same drawing otherwise, so legs and catwalk thin down with it.
3Option 2’s outline, plus: legs and riser drawn as round steel columns (riser the heavier) and shaded like the tank; the catwalk as a solid deck with its dark underside, and a handrail above it on posts (a mid-rail too at 192 and 512).
4Option 3 plus riveted plate seams on the tank, the roof and the bowl. For the 512 splash only.

Help, About: 48 px, the size it really is

The mark is 1.5em of a 2rem heading, 48 CSS px. The row below it is the same three marks at twice that, only so the difference is easy to see.

App shortcut icon: 192 px

icon-192 is declared “any”: never cropped, so it keeps the favicon’s full-bleed framing and the 80% circle does not apply to it.

Splash screen and installed icon: 512 px

icon-512 and icon.svg are “maskable”, so a phone may crop them to any shape; only the middle 80% circle is promised. Every option keeps the shipped framing: the body scaled 0.85 inside that circle, the legs and riser run to the true bottom edge.

512, cropped to the 80% circle (the worst a phone may do), shown at 256

The red ring is the 80% circle. The legs crossing it is by design (they reach the ground uncropped); the body stays inside it in every option.

Not asked for: two things the thin outline makes possible, each against option 4

A · Task 645, shorter legs. The heavy outline is what forced the body down to 0.85, and that is why the app icon’s legs are leggier than the favicon’s. With the thin outline the body can grow to 0.93 and keep the same margin the shipped icon really has. Leg-to-tank ratio: favicon 0.94, shipped 1.15, this 1.03.

B · Sway bracing. A strut and tie-rod crosses between the legs, which is most of what makes a hometown tower look like one.
