Roof shading, before and after (2026-09-28)

Tom on the larger-tower comparison: option 3 for the About box and 192, option 4 with the bracing and the rivets for the 512 splash — “I agree. My only question is about the roof shading. Is it right to be plain white?” and, new, “it shouldn’t be bright under the catwalk. That should be in shadow.”

The wall’s own gradient says the light is upper-left (bright at 0.36 of the way across, darkest at the right edge). BEFORE, the roof ignored that and used a near-white radial fill that never gets darker than a pale grey — a flat cone under a directional light. AFTER, the roof reuses the wall’s own gradient, unchanged, over its own outline, so the same lit-left/shaded-right split continues up through the roof. Separately, the bowl’s own gradient is brightest at its centre, which sits directly under the catwalk deck; AFTER adds a cast shadow there instead.

About box mark, 48 px (option 3)

Changed: the roof fill and a shadow wash on the bowl top. Why: at 48 px the mark is tiny, so the About box is exactly where a flat-white roof and a bright deck underside would be least noticed and most wrong — this is the size Tom is judging.

App icon, 192 px (option 3)

Changed: same roof and bowl-top fix as the About mark, scaled to 192’s own stroke weights. Why: shown again at 48 to check the shading still reads once the icon is shrunk to home-screen size, not just at its native 192.

Splash screen / icon-512 (option 4 + bracing)

Changed: the same roof-and-bowl fix, plus the riveted seams keep their own shading unchanged. Why shown at 32: shading built for a 512 px splash can turn to mud once shrunk this far, and the roof/bowl fix has to still read as shading, not noise, at that size.
