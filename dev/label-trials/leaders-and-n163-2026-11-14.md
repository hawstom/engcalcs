## What the map draws today (survey, 2026-11-14)

Rendered Novato (9 and 12 notches about N163), Net3 and the 600-junction network; counted on the DOM stub over
64 views and in Chromium (`label-leader-shapes-browser-harness.js`). The stub lays a stack out as one row, so
everything about stacks comes from Chromium.

| fault a drafter would not draw | count in Chromium (14 views) |
|---|---|
| leader runs more than 8 px longer than the way from its node to the nearest row of its label | 468 of 1,890 (25%) |
| leader crosses a pipe that is not its own | 383 of 1,890 (20%) |
| leader through another node's symbol (within 5 px of its centre) | 54-98 (mostly 20 px node spacing; no clean fix) |
| leader across another leader | 1-3 |
| leader through another label's row | 0 (`dropStruckLeaders()` already refuses these) |
| leader longer than 120 px, bent, or ending at no label | 0-1 |

The first is the "far corner": a stack hangs down from its leader's end, so the leader meets the first row
whatever side of the stack the node is on. Examples: a 72 px leader to a stack whose lowest row stood 19 px
from its node; over Novato the median leader ran 6 px longer than it had to and the top tenth 30 px longer.

## Kept: a stack's leader meets the near edge of the label, on the 15-degree grid

`attachToNearestRow()` in `dropStruckLeaders()`, flag `LPN_LEADER_NEAREST_ROW`. The leader still ends on the label's near
edge, but at the point where a ray from the node at one of `Collide.RING_ANGLES` (multiples of 15, never level or plumb:
Tom, 2026-08-16, goal 7 in `label-placement-goals.md`) meets that edge inside a drawn row. The shortest such leader that
crosses no label row, no drawn leader, no symbol and no other box of the drawing, and no more pipes than the leader it
replaces, is taken; the old leader (to the first row, on the grid by how its side was chosen) stays when there is none. Only the
leader's end moves: no label is placed, hidden or shortened by it (same node labels, pipe labels and pipe fields with it on and off).

- **The first build ended the leader at a row's baseline and left the grid** (Perry's pre-review, real Chromium: node
  leaders more than 1.5 degrees off the grid 42 -> 243 of about 790 on Novato). Now, over the harness's 26 views: node leaders off the
  grid 87 -> 81, within 5 degrees of level or plumb 6 -> 6, leaders running long 718 -> 506 (-30%), leaders across a pipe 496 -> 491.
- The end is stored as a distance down the edge in row pitches (a fraction) and the label's row count, drawn with the label's own
  font size, so a zoom frame keeps it on its ray (`refreshFontSizes()` moves the drawn end with the font). It is kept when an edit with
  Recalculate off rewrites the label with the same number of rows (an elevation edit: 47.4 px below the first row's top before and
  after), and cleared when the rows change number, and for a label the user moved. Banked with the zoom bucket.
- Rows are counted with blank ones.
- A project at 1e7 ft: coordinates are local to the file's origin, so the drawing numbers stay small; the harness runs Net3 with
  origin 1e7 and gets the same leaders set to meet a row as near the origin. A project whose *drawing numbers* were 1e7 would not
  draw labels where they belong in any case (every leader end 10 px off its row, rule off and on): the renderer's float32.

## N163 on Novato: the second try for ties is NOT kept

A tie (a stack that shows everything, where a line shows the same) was tried for its line once, while its neighbours were still
stacks standing on the ground. Tried a second time after they have taken their lines (`LPN_TIE_RETRY`, built and removed), it puts
N163 on its line where master has a stack, **and it costs pipe labels**. Measured with a real mouse wheel turned over N163 from
Zoom to fit, Novato's own label settings, notch 0 = the fit (a scratch bench, not kept), master against the branch with the retry:

| window | master shows N163 as a stack at | the retry makes it a line at | pipe labels master shows, hurt by the retry |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1920 x 1000 | 1-3, 11-15, 17-18 | 12-15, 17-18 | L251 3 -> 1 field at 1 and 2; L233 and L317 hidden at 5; L180 3 -> 1 at 13 |
| 1600 x 900 | 3-5, 13-18 | 13-17 | L251 3 -> 1 at 4; L317 hidden at 7; L181 hidden at 13 |
| 1366 x 768 | 0-1, 6-8, 16-18 | 17-18 | L180 3 -> 1 at 17 and 18 |

With the retry off the branch loses nothing against master at any of these notches (1920 x 1000: 0 hidden, 0 fields lost).
**Master already shows N163 as one line for about seven notches in every window** (4-10 at 1920, 6-12 at 1600, 9-15 at 1366). The
earlier claim "a stack at 12 to 16 on master" held only in a view centred on N163, which is not what a wheel makes. The retry
changes a stack to a line (same values) at the cost of values on pipe labels, so it was dropped; the code is in `151aa16fa`.
What holds N163 as a stack at 11 to 15 notches in the centred 1920 view (`whyNode('163')`, 256 sides within 8 text heights): 108-118
node symbols, 20-47 pipe labels (L181, L180, L179, L116, L321, L221, L177), 7-14 the labels of N161 and N164; 4-11 free and none of
them a line beside its neighbours.
