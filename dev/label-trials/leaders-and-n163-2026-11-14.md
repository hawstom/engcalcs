## What the map draws today (survey, 2026-11-14)

Rendered Novato (9 and 12 notches about N163), Net3 and the 600-junction network; counted on the DOM stub over
64 views (Net3 1x-8x, Novato 1x-8x, n600 1x-4x, Novato notches 0-16) and in Chromium over 14 views
(`label-leader-shapes-browser-harness.js`). About 2,400 leaders drawn on the 64 stub views; 1,900 on the 14
Chromium views. The stub lays a stack out as one row, so everything about stacks comes from Chromium.

| fault a drafter would not draw | count in Chromium (14 views) |
|---|---|
| leader runs more than 8 px longer than the way from its node to the nearest row of its label | 468 of 1,890 (25%) |
| leader crosses a pipe that is not its own | 383 of 1,890 (20%) |
| leader through another node's symbol (within 5 px of its centre) | 54-98 (mostly 20 px node spacing; no clean fix) |
| leader across another leader | 1-3 |
| leader through another label's row | 0 (`dropStruckLeaders()` already refuses these) |
| leader longer than 120 px, bent, or ending at no label | 0-1 |

The first is the "far corner": a stack hangs down from its leader's end, so the leader meets the first row
whatever side of the stack the node is on. Examples: a 72 px leader to a stack whose lowest row stood 19 px
from its node; over Novato the median leader ran 6 px longer than it had to and the top tenth 30 px longer.

## Kept

**1. A stack's leader meets the row nearest its node** (`attachToNearestRow()` in `dropStruckLeaders()`,
`LPN_LEADER_NEAREST_ROW`). Only the leader's end slides down the label's near edge; no label moves, so none is
hidden or shown by it (the same labels, values and pipe fields as without it). It stays on the first row if the
shorter leader would cross another label's row, another leader, another node's symbol, or more pipes than the
leader it replaces. Banked with the zoom bucket (`leaderAttach`), or a revisit drew another picture
(`label-bucket-cache-harness.js` caught it). Chromium: leaders running long 468 -> 334 (-29%).
**Needs Tom's call:** these leaders are no longer on the 15-degree grid (his 2026-08-16 call, "constrain leader
angles to be at multiples of 15 degrees and not orthogonal", `label-placement-goals.md` goal 7). A leader to a
lower row can be any angle. `LPN_LEADER_NEAREST_ROW = false` puts it back.

**2. A stack that shows everything is tried for its line a second time** (`LPN_TIE_RETRY`). N163 on Novato was
a tie (stack and line show the same values), and ties were tried once, while its neighbours were still stacks
standing on the ground a line needs. Tried again after they have taken their lines, N163 is a line at 12, 13,
14, 15 and 16 notches (Chromium; at 11 it stays a stack). No pipe field changes at those notches. The earlier
"retry measured: no gain" was on the DOM stub, where N163 is already a line, so the stub cannot see this.

## After the code review (2026-11-14, same branch)

- A leader's row is kept as an **index** (and the label's row count), not a distance: the end is drawn that many rows of the
  label's own font size below the first baseline, so a zoom frame (which rewrites the font before the pass) and the rescale
  loops draw it on its row; `refreshFontSizes()` moves the drawn end with it. It is cleared when the rows are rewritten
  (`writeNodeLabelGlyphs()`, so an edit with Recalculate off that cuts 3 rows to 1 draws an ordinary leader) and for a
  label the user moved. No float equality on positions any more.
- Rows are counted with blank ones: the first drawn row's index, not its place among the boxes.
- The shorter leader is also tested against every other box of the drawing (Text objects, pipe labels along their pipes,
  level labels, dragged labels, customer boxes), and leaders already struck (never drawn) no longer veto it.
- One walk of the segments counts the pipes both leaders cross (was two closures per leader).
- A project at 1e7 ft: coordinates are local to the file's origin, so the drawing numbers stay small and the origin is far; the
  harness runs Net3 with origin 1e7 and gets the same 4, 10 and 10 leaders set to meet a row as near the origin. A project whose
  *drawing numbers* were 1e7 would not draw labels where they belong in any case (every leader end 10 px off its row, in the
  rule off and on): the renderer's float32, nothing in this rule.
- The time assertion left the harness (load-dependent); the bench measures time.
